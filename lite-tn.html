Batlamedi

Orange Botswana
Mobile · 43% of mobile subscriptions (BOCRA-published share)
Botswana's largest mobile operator by subscriber share as of March 2024 — the first time Orange has led the market since launch. Majority-owned (69%) by Orange S.A. (France), with the remainder held by a local investor consortium.

Mascom Wireless
Mobile · 42% of mobile subscriptions (BOCRA-published share)
Botswana's second-largest mobile operator by subscriber share as of March 2024. Owned 60% by the Botswana Public Officers Pensions Fund and 40% by Econet Wireless. Was the first operator to launch 5G in Botswana (Gaborone, February 2022).

BTC Mobile (BeMobile)
Mobile · active mobile subscribers (company-reported, FY2026)
State-linked operator (majority government-owned via the BSE-listed Botswana Telecommunications Corporation) held 15% of the mobile subscriber market per BOCRA as of March 2024. Its own FY2026 results separately reported 757,529 active mobile subscribers, up 30% year-on-year — a later reporting period than the BOCRA share figure, so the two numbers aren't directly comparable.

Starlink
Fixed/Wireless · satellite broadband customers
Satellite internet service, commercially launched in Botswana in August 2024 after BOCRA licensing. Reported at roughly 8,075 customers (about 3.9% of fixed broadband connections) as of BOCRA's most recent annual report, via press reporting — not independently verified against the primary BOCRA table.

Independent ISPs
Fixed/Wireless · no public subscriber count
BOCRA licenses a number of smaller independent ISPs beyond the operators above (fixed, wireless and corporate connectivity). No combined subscriber or market-share figure for this group is published. BoFiNet, Botswana's wholesale national fibre backbone operator, isn't listed separately here as it has no retail subscribers of its own — it supplies capacity to the operators above.
